Test Case ID: UI-001
Title: Check that a room can be booked with valid data

Preconditions:
- The application is available at https://automationintesting.online/.
- A room has an available future date range.
- Check Out date is after Check In date.

Test Data:
- Firstname: Alex
- Lastname: Tester
- Email: qa.manual@example.com
- Phone: 01234567890

Steps:
1. Open the home page.
2. Enter available dates in Check In and Check Out.
3. Click Check Availability.
4. Click Book now for the chosen room.
5. Check the selected dates in the room calendar and click Reserve Now.
6. Fill Firstname, Lastname, Email and Phone with the test data.
7. Click Reserve Now to submit the booking.

Expected Result:
- Booking Confirmed is displayed.
- The confirmation shows the selected check-in and check-out dates.

Test Case ID: UI-003
Title: Check that previously booked dates are shown as unavailable

Preconditions:
- A confirmed booking exists for a known room and future date range.
- The booked room and dates are known.
- The same room has another available date range.

Steps:
1. Open the home page.
2. Enter a different available date range that does not overlap the existing booking.
3. Click Check Availability.
4. Click Book now for the same room.
5. Use Back or Next in the room calendar if needed to open the month with the booked dates.
6. Check how the previously booked dates are shown.

Expected Result:
- The previously booked date range is visibly marked as unavailable in the room calendar.
